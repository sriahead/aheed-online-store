# Promotion edit page keeps its saved bundle, code or product (requirements)

R1. On the edit page, a promotion linked to a switched-off bundle lists that bundle, marked switched off.
    Opening and saving the promotion keeps the link.
R2. The same applies to an inactive discount code (marked "(inactive)") and to a product no longer on offer.
R3. Choosing a department never removes the currently chosen product from the product picker.
R4. Under the Starts/Ends fields, a hint says blank dates run the promotion until it is switched off.
