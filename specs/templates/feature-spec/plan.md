# REPLACE ME — Feature Name (plan)

The narrative: why this slice exists, what it proves, and where its edges are. `requirements.md`
holds the checkable acceptance criteria; this file holds the reasoning a reader needs to trust
those requirements are the right ones — the thing a numbered list can't carry on its own.

**Goal:** one or two sentences. What does shipping this slice actually prove or unblock?

**Scope (this slice):**
- What's actually being built, in enough detail that "why R3 says what it says" is obvious once
  you've read this section. Reference real files/paths, not abstractions.

**Deliberately excluded:**
- Anything a reasonable reader would expect but isn't here — and why. This is the section that
  prevents silent scope creep and stops the same "is X in scope?" question from being re-asked.

**Open items carried forward** (if any): things this slice can't resolve — usually because they
need a human decision, an external credential, or a prerequisite slice that hasn't shipped yet.
Cross-reference the issue tracking each one.
