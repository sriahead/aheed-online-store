# Merqify Platform Terms — Merchant Services Schedule

> **IMPORTANT NOTICE:**  
> This draft schedule must undergo formal legal review by qualified UK commercial solicitors prior to merchant execution and publication.

---

## 1. Nature of Platform Services

1.1 **Software Provider Only.** Merqify Ltd ("the Company", "Platform Provider") provides cloud-based e-commerce software, catalogue management interfaces, inventory synchronization, order management tools, and online storefront hosting ("the Services") to independent retailers and grocery merchants ("the Merchant").

1.2 **Independent Merchant Status.** The Merchant acts as an independent principal and seller of goods. The Merchant contracts directly with shoppers and consumers. Nothing in this Agreement or through the provision of the Services creates a partnership, joint venture, principal-agent, employer-employee, or fiduciary relationship between the Company and the Merchant.

---

## 2. Product Listings, Classifications & VAT Compliance

2.1 **Merchant Sole Responsibility.** The Merchant is solely responsible for:
- Establishing, verifying, and maintaining all product listings, descriptions, categorizations, and retail prices;
- Determining and assigning the appropriate Value Added Tax (VAT) rate and classification (including standard rate, reduced rate, zero rate, or exempt treatment) for every product listed on the Merchant's storefront in strict accordance with the Value Added Tax Act 1994, HMRC VAT Notice 701/14 (Food), and all applicable UK tax legislation;
- Accounting for, reporting, and remitting all applicable sales taxes, VAT, and duties to HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC).

2.2 **No Tax or Accounting Advice.** The Company does not provide tax, legal, financial, or accounting advice. Neither the Services nor any documentation, communications, or automated platform features provided by the Company constitute tax guidance.

2.3 **Guidance Tools & Automated Discrepancy Warnings.** The Services may include automated validation tools, heuristic rule checks, and visual discrepancy warnings (e.g. flagging potential mismatches between spreadsheet entries and standard HMRC categories). The Merchant expressly acknowledges and agrees that:
- Any such automated checks, flags, indicators, or suggestions are provided solely for operational convenience and basic information;
- The Company makes no warranty or representation as to the accuracy, completeness, or statutory compliance of any automated suggestion or heuristic check;
- The provision of such warnings does not replace, diminish, or discharge the Merchant's statutory obligation to obtain independent qualified tax advice and verify the correct VAT treatment of every product.

---

## 3. Indemnity & Limitation of Liability

3.1 **Tax Indemnity.** The Merchant shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the Company, its directors, officers, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, assessments, penalties, fines, interest, liabilities, losses, or legal costs arising out of or in connection with:
- Any incorrect VAT classification, calculation, or omission by the Merchant;
- Any misrepresentation of product categories or tax rates;
- Any dispute, audit, or enforcement action brought by HMRC or any taxation authority in connection with the Merchant's sales through the platform.

3.2 **Limitation of Liability.** To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the Company shall have no liability whatsoever for any tax penalties, assessments, lost profits, or consequential damages resulting from the Merchant's reliance on the platform tools, catalogue imports, or automated VAT indicators.
