# Admin Panel Guide

Welcome to the **Admin Panel**. As a store manager or administrator, you have access to advanced configuration tabs to manage the business logic and presentation of the storefront.

## Orders & Fulfillment
While staff handle picking, you can view the macroscopic order flow, issue full refunds, and monitor SLA compliance.

## Catalogue & Categories
- **Catalogue**: Manage product listings, update prices (always in integer pence), upload imagery, and tweak SEO metadata.
- **Categories**: Organize the storefront hierarchy (e.g., *Fresh Produce*, *HMC Halal Meat*) to help customers navigate.

## Storefront Configuration
Customize the visual identity of your online store. Update the logo, change brand colors, and edit the promotional banner at the top of the homepage.

## Loyalty & Discounts
- **Loyalty**: Configure point multipliers and redemption rates for the customer rewards program.
- **Discounts**: Create promotional codes (e.g., `SUMMER10`), set usage limits, and define minimum cart thresholds.

## Reports & Team
- **Reports**: View aggregate sales data, best-selling items, and revenue over time.
- **Team**: Invite new staff members and manage their roles (Staff vs. Admin) securely.
