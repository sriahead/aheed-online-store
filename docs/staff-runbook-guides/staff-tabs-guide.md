# Staff Panel Guide

Welcome to the **Staff Panel**. As a staff member, your primary interface contains three main tabs designed to help you run day-to-day shop-floor operations.

## 1. Overview
The **Overview** tab provides a quick snapshot of the current shift. Here you will see:
- Urgent pending orders that need picking.
- Recent alerts about low stock items.
- Quick links to common actions.

## 2. Live Inventory & Availability
The **Live Inventory & Availability** tab is where you manage what's currently on the shelves.
- **Honest Stock**: Only mark items as out-of-stock when they are truly gone.
- **Adjustments**: Update quantities if you spot discrepancies during your shift.
- **Back-in-Stock**: Rapidly toggle availability for high-turnover items like fresh produce.

## 3. Fulfillment & Orders
The **Fulfillment & Orders** tab is your workstation for handling customer orders.
- **Picking**: View items required for new orders and pick them from the shop floor.
- **Status Updates**: Transition orders from *Confirmed* → *Out for Delivery* → *Delivered*.
- **Exceptions**: Handle substitutions or refunds if an item is missing during picking.
