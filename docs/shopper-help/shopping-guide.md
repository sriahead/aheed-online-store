# Shopping Guide

Welcome! This guide explains how to find what you need, place orders, and make the most of your shopping experience.

## Browsing & Finding Products
- **Homepage:** Check the top banner and featured products carousel for the latest offers.
- **Categories:** Use the menu to browse by category (e.g., Fresh Produce, Meat).
- **Product Details:** Click on any product to see more information, including whether it is Organic, Fresh, or Halal. You can also read reviews left by other customers.
- **Search:** Use the search bar to find specific items quickly.

## Cart & Checkout
- **Adding to Cart:** Adjust the quantity and click "Add to Cart". Your cart will save your items while you continue shopping.
- **Guest Checkout:** You can checkout quickly as a guest without creating an account.
- **Delivery Rules:** 
  - Some stores have a minimum order amount.
  - A delivery fee may apply, but you might qualify for free delivery if your order is above a certain amount.
  - Delivery is restricted to specific postcode areas.
- **Discounts:** If you have a promotional code, you can apply it in your cart before paying.

## Managing Your Orders
- **Order Status:** After placing an order, its status will update from *Pending Payment* to *Confirmed*. When the store prepares it, it will change to *Out for Delivery*, and finally *Delivered*.
- **Order History:** If you create an account, you can view your full past order history and track active orders.
- **Cancellations:** If you need to cancel an order, please contact the store directly.

## Loyalty Program (If Enabled)
If the store has opted into the rewards program:
- **Earning Points:** You earn points for every pound spent.
- **Redeeming Points:** Once you reach the minimum point threshold, you can apply your points at checkout for a discount.
- **Tiers:** High-spending customers may be placed into loyalty tiers (like Silver or Gold), which grant point multipliers so you earn rewards faster!
- **Expiry:** Note that points may expire if your account is inactive for a certain number of months.

## Ratings & Reviews
After trying a product, you can leave a 1 to 5-star rating and a written review to help other shoppers!
