# Staff Daily Operations Playbook

Welcome to the **Staff Panel**. As a staff member, your primary interface contains three main tabs designed to help you run day-to-day shop-floor operations.

## 1. Fulfillment & Orders
The **Fulfillment & Orders** tab is your workstation for handling customer orders.
- **Order States:** You are responsible for moving orders from *Confirmed* (paid and ready to pick), to *Out for Delivery*, and finally *Delivered*.
- **Picking:** View items required for new orders and pick them from the shop floor.
- **Exceptions:** If a customer cancels an order or payment fails, it will move to the *Cancelled* state.
- **Customer Lookup:** If a customer calls about an order, you can search for their order using either their **Email Address** or **Order Number**. *(Note: Rate limits apply to order lookups to ensure security).*

## 2. Live Inventory & Availability
The **Live Inventory & Availability** tab is where you manage what's currently on the shelves.
- **Honest Stock:** Only mark items as out-of-stock when they are truly gone. The system automatically reduces stock when orders are placed.
- **Low Stock Alerts:** Items that drop below their defined low-stock threshold will be flagged so you know what needs restocking.
- **Adjustments:** Update quantities manually if you spot discrepancies during your shift.

## 3. Overview
The **Overview** tab provides a quick snapshot of the current shift. Here you will see:
- Urgent pending orders that need picking.
- Recent alerts about low stock items.
- Quick links to common actions.

## Customer Data & Loyalty
- **Loyalty Points:** Customer loyalty points are earned and deducted automatically during checkout. You do not need to manage these manually.
- **Security:** Remember that customer data (like addresses and emails) is strictly confidential. Only search for orders when directly assisting a customer.
