# Merqify Knowledge Management System (KMS)

Welcome to **Merqify KMS**, the central, authoritative source of truth for the entire **Merqify** ecosystem. 

Merqify is a multi-tenant retail and grocery e-commerce platform powering production storefronts for our clients, including **Aheed Food Centre** and **SRIMART**.

---

## 🏛️ System & Client Context

- **Platform Core:** Merqify (PostgreSQL-first, multi-tenant, edge runtime on Cloudflare Workers + Neon Postgres).
- **Current Trading Clients:**
  - **Aheed Food Centre** (`aheed.merqify.com`, `aheedfoodcentre.co.uk`) — Fresh produce, halal meat, grocery delivery, and Click & Collect in Milton Keynes.
  - **SRIMART** (`srimart.merqify.com`) — Retail grocery delivery in Reading.
- **Knowledge Governance:** Single source of truth with strict role-based access control (RBAC), tenant isolation, and link-integrity enforcement.

## 🧭 Navigation by Knowledge Track

The portal is organised into two primary operational portals:

### 🏬 [Client Help & Documentation Centre](/staff)
*For Store Owners, Store Administrators, Operational Staff, and Client Stakeholders.*

A practical, task-oriented knowledge base designed to configure, launch, operate, and troubleshoot client store operations:

1. **[Getting Started & Store Setup](/staff/guide/getting-started-onboarding)** — Store branding, business hours, postal delivery outcodes, Stripe Connect payouts, and launch verification.
2. **[Role Journey Maps](/staff/guide/client-user-journeys)** — Step-by-step journeys, route shortcuts, and verification checklists for Platform Admins, Store Admins, Staff, and Customers.
3. **[Catalogue & Inventory Management](/staff/guide/products-catalogue-guide)** — Product catalogue creation, Excel spreadsheet imports, barcodes, stock pricing, and category hierarchies.
4. **[Store Operations & Order Fulfilment](/staff/runbook/order-fulfilment-core)** — Canonical order lifecycle runbook (`CONFIRMED` → `PREPARING` → `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` / `READY_FOR_COLLECTION` → `DELIVERED`), packing SOPs, and delivery dispatches.
5. **[Staff Playbook & Shift Operations](/staff/runbook/staff-tabs-guide)** — Daily operations, live inventory out-of-stock toggles, and customer review moderation.
6. **[Store Admin Management Guide](/staff/runbook/store-admin-tabs-guide)** — Managing promotions, discount codes, customer loyalty points, staff team roles, and in-app customer FAQs.
7. **[Platform Administration & Multi-Tenant Management](/staff/guide/platform-admin-guide)** — Tenant provisioning, domain routing, Neon database round-trip telemetry, and AI token budget governance.
8. **[Roles, Permissions & Tenant Security](/staff/guide/roles-permissions-matrix)** — Role capability matrix, tenant data isolation (`vendorId` boundaries), and access troubleshooting.
9. **[Troubleshooting & Escalation Matrix](/staff/runbook/troubleshooting-escalation)** — Symptom-to-solution matrices, payment exception recoveries, and 4-tier escalation paths.
10. **[Documentation Governance & Lifecycle](/staff/guide/documentation-management)** — Document ownership, review cadences, metadata standards, and knowledge change control.
