# Client Help & Documentation Centre

Welcome to the Client Knowledge Centre. This portal provides complete, verified guides and standard operating procedures (SOPs) to help store owners, administrators, and staff independently configure, launch, operate, and troubleshoot their stores.

## 2. 🗺️ User Journey Maps
- **[Complete Role User Journey Maps](/staff/guide/client-user-journeys)** — Visual flows, decision gates, required permissions, and step sequences for all platform roles:

  - **Vendor Admin Journey:** Initial onboarding, branding customization, catalogue creation, delivery slots, and launch audit.

## 6. 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Support
- **[Store Troubleshooting & Escalation Matrix](/staff/runbook/troubleshooting-escalation)** — Diagnostic matrix mapping symptoms to step-by-step solutions:
  - **Product Import Errors:** Missing barcodes, duplicate codes (`23505`), invalid currency formatting.
  - **Payment Failures:** Stripe payment rejections, card authentication timeouts, webhook delays.
  - **Delivery Outcode Rejections:** Unrecognized postal outcodes, address outside delivery zone.
  - **Order State Locks:** Resolving stuck orders and processing manual refunds.
- **[4-Tier Support Escalation Path](/staff/runbook/troubleshooting-escalation#support-escalation-tiers)** — Escalation pathways from Store Staff to Store Admin, Platform Admin, and Core Engineering.
- **[Support Contacts & Operational Channels](/staff/runbook/troubleshooting-escalation#support-contacts--channels)** — Official WhatsApp operations contact and issue reporting.
