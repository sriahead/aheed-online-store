# Internal Project & Engineering Knowledge

Welcome to the internal engineering and architectural documentation repository for the Aheed platform. This portal serves as the single source of truth for software engineers, systems architects, DevOps, and product stakeholders.

> [!NOTE]
> **Access Restriction:** Internal Engineering Documentation. Content here is single-sourced from `specs/`, `docs/`, and `CLAUDE.md`, and is restricted behind Cloudflare Access.

## 5. 🚀 Milestone Engineering Plans (M0–P10)
Over 200 granular engineering specifications and delivery plans documenting every feature slice:
- **Milestone 0 (M0):** Walking Skeleton, CI/CD, basic health checks.
- **Phase 0–1 (P0–P1):** Scaffolding, Better Auth email/password, Google OAuth, RBAC foundation.
- **Phase 2 (P2–P2.6):** Catalogue browsing, full-text search, visual redesign, ratings & reviews.
- **Phase 3–4 (P3–P4):** Shopping cart, slot booking, delivery rules, checkout pipeline.
- **Phase 5–7 (P5–P7):** Stripe Connect payment processing, staff operations panel, audit compliance.
- **Phase 8–9 (P8–P9):** Multi-tenant custom domains, live production readiness, automated regression hardening.
- **Phase 10 (P10):** Post-launch improvements, 3D icon suite, Click & Collect order communications, and Help Centre KMS integration.
